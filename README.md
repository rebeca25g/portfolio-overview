## 🔍 Project Summaries

### 1. Accounts Payable Invoice Analysis

Analyzed **5,000 simulated AP invoices** to identify inefficiencies in the invoice lifecycle. Uncovered that **19.7% of invoices were blocked**, with duplicate invoices and incorrect coding as the leading root causes. Built an interactive Power BI dashboard covering payment performance, vendor analysis, and approval bottlenecks.

**Key finding:** The Finance department's approval time (~7.5 days) was **67% longer than Procurement**, making it the primary processing bottleneck.

### 2. Ride-Hailing Operations Analysis

Analyzed **50,000 Uber trip records** across six U.S. cities (Jan–Feb 2023) to surface demand patterns, cancellation drivers, and city-level revenue concentration. Built a two-page Power BI dashboard covering revenue and trip operations.

**Key findings:**
- **~15% of trips never completed** (10% cancellations + 5% no-shows), representing roughly **$119K in unrealized revenue** over five weeks — the single largest operational inefficiency surfaced.
- Demand follows a clear **twin-peak daily rhythm** (7–8 AM and 5–7 PM), with Saturday the weekly peak.
- **New York, Los Angeles, and Chicago generate ~65% of total revenue**, with NYC alone at ~27%.

[View project →](https://github.com/rebeca25g/Uber-rides-analysis)

### 3. Berlin Airbnb Market Analysis

Analyzed **14,274 Airbnb listings across Berlin's 12 districts** to understand how price varies geographically, who controls the supply, and how Berlin's short-term-rental regulation surfaces in the data. Built a five-page Power BI dashboard with a custom Berlin districts choropleth map.

**Key findings:**
- **Mitte commands the price premium** (~€156/night average) versus outer districts like Marzahn-Hellersdorf (~€97), with both price and review activity concentrating centrally.
- The market is **more commercial than peer-to-peer**: 43.4% of listings belong to multi-listing hosts, and the single largest operator runs 311 listings.
- **Regulation leaves a visible fingerprint:** the largest minimum-stay bucket is **31+ nights (~5,480 listings)**, consistent with listings structured as longer-term rentals within Berlin's short-term-let rules.

[View project →](https://github.com/rebeca25g/Airbnb-Berlin-Market-Analysis)
